Card Summoner AR

Register a card with a photo, pick a creature, then point your camera at it.

Photo tips: lay the card flat in good light, shoot straight down, fill the frame with the card, avoid glare on the foil. The artwork box is the most trackable part.
Custom model: choose "Custom .glb" and load any GLB file. It's auto-scaled to stand on the card, and its first animation loops.
Tap the screen in AR to make visible creatures attack.
